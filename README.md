# InkCraft — Tablet Stylus Note-Taking Studio

**InkCraft** is a native Android tablet note-taking app engineered from the ground up for **active styluses** (Samsung S Pen, USI, Lenovo Precision Pen, Xiaomi Smart Pen).

## Download for Tablet
Grab the latest signed `.apk` directly from **[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/AchieverSince2004/Performance/releases/latest)** on your Android tablet and tap to install.

## Key Features
- **Pro Stylus Ink Engine**: 120/240Hz historical motion sampling, true pressure sensitivity & tilt dynamics across **Fountain Pen**, **Ballpoint**, **Fineliner**, **Brush Pen**, **Highlighter**, and **Laser Pointer**.
- **Smart Palm Rejection & Pen Hardware**: One-tap **Stylus-Only** mode lets fingers pan and pinch-zoom without leaving stray marks; supports stylus flip-eraser and side-button quick erase.
- **Hold-to-Snap Shape Recognition**: Draw rough lines, circles, ellipses, rectangles, or triangles and hold briefly to snap into crisp vector shapes.
- **Noteshelf-Style Zoom Box**: Magnified close-up writing strip at the bottom of the screen with line-advance controls for super-neat handwriting.
- **Straightedge Ruler & Lasso Tool**: Snap strokes to an interactive ruler, or circle strokes with the Lasso tool to move, recolor, or delete.
- **10+ Paper Templates & 5 Paper Colors**: Blank, Ruled (Wide/Narrow), Cornell Notes, Dot Grid, Square Grid, Isometric Grid, Music Staff, Daily Planner, and Weekly Schedule on Crisp White, Warm Cream, Soft Sepia, Dark Slate, or Midnight paper.
- **PDF Annotation & Export**: Import multi-page PDFs to annotate directly on top, insert photos and sticky notes, record lecture audio memos, and export full notebooks to PDF.
- **Dockable Left/Right/Top Toolbar & Pomodoro Timer**: Built for both left-handed and right-handed tablet writers.
